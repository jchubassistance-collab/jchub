You already sell to people around you, on WhatsApp or at the market, and you want to take things further online? Good news: to get started, you need neither a big budget nor a developer. You need a clear identity, an easy way to be reached, good photos and clean documents. Here are the free tools for each step.

## 1. A recognizable identity

A customer should recognize your shop at a glance, on your WhatsApp profile as on your packaging.

- **Your logo**: if you already have one, even imperfect, polish it with [JcHub Logo Studio](/en/tools/logo-studio): colors, transparent background, outline, cropping. The tool also creates an icon pack and your future website’s favicon.
- **Your colors**: pick two or three colors and use them everywhere. The [color converter](/en/tools/color-converter) gives you their exact codes (HEX, RGB) to reuse them without mistakes.

## 2. Be easy to reach

In many countries, **WhatsApp is the first sales channel**. Make it easy:

- Switch to **WhatsApp Business**, which is free: business profile, opening hours, product catalog, quick replies.
- Create a **QR code** that opens a chat with you directly, with the [QR code generator](/en/tools/qr-code-generator). The link to encode looks like `https://wa.me/234XXXXXXXXXX`, with your full number, no “+” and no spaces. Print it on your cards, your counter, your bags.

## 3. Photos that make people want to buy

A good photo sells more than a long description.

- Shoot in **daylight**, on a **plain background**, product well centered.
- Resize your images for each network with the [image resizer and cropper](/en/tools/social-image-resizer-cropper): square for Instagram, landscape for a Facebook banner.
- Make them lighter with the [WebP / PNG optimizer](/en/tools/webp-png-image-optimizer) before posting: they’ll load faster for customers on 3G or 4G.

## 4. Professional documents

A clean invoice reassures customers and avoids misunderstandings.

- The [invoice and quote generator](/en/tools/invoice-generator) creates a PDF with your logo, in the currency you use, with automatic calculations.
- A PDF too heavy for WhatsApp or email? The [PDF compressor](/en/tools/compress-pdf) shrinks it sharply.
- To digitize your paper order forms, gather their photos into one file with [Images to PDF](/en/tools/images-to-pdf).

## 5. Get found on Google

When someone searches for “fabric shop near me” or “caterer in town”, you want to show up.

- Create your **Google Business Profile** (formerly Google My Business), which is free: address, hours, photos, number, customer reviews. It can appear in Google Maps and in search results.
- Ask happy customers to leave a **review**: it’s what reassures a new customer the most.

## 6. A storefront on social media

A **Facebook page** or a **professional Instagram account** costs nothing and gives you a visible storefront. Post regularly: new arrivals, behind the scenes, customer reviews. Consistency matters more than perfection.

## Customer service, your best free tool

Tools don’t replace good habits. **Answer fast**: a customer left without a reply for an hour often buys elsewhere; WhatsApp Business quick replies help you keep that pace. **Show your prices** when you can, or at least a range: many customers don’t dare to ask. And **confirm every order in writing**, with the amount, the delay and the delivery method.

## Where to start this week

1. Monday: switch to WhatsApp Business and fill in your profile.
2. Tuesday: polish your logo and pick your colors.
3. Wednesday: photograph your 10 best products.
4. Thursday: create your WhatsApp QR code and print it.
5. Friday: open your Google Business Profile.

Five small actions, zero spent, and your shop already looks serious.

## Key takeaways

- WhatsApp Business and a QR code are enough to start selling online.
- Good photos, light and in the right format, sell more.
- A clean invoice reassures your customers.
- Google Business Profile is free and helps you get found.
