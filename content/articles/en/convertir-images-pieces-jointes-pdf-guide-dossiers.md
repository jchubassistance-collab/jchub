# Convert Your Images and Attachments to PDF: A Complete Guide for Flawless Documents

Whether you are submitting an administrative request, sending a job application, submitting expense reports, or uploading supporting documents to a bank, sending multiple files as separate photos (JPG, PNG) is a frequent cause of rejections or delays. Submission platforms and professional recipients almost universally prefer the PDF format for its layout stability and universal compatibility.

This practical guide details the best methods to scan, organize, and bundle your images into a single, perfectly legible PDF document ready to send.

---

## Why Group Your Images into a PDF?

Sending multiple individual image files creates several practical issues:

- **Clutter and lost order**: documents sent as multiple attachments often arrive in an arbitrary order on the recipient's machine.
- **Uncontrolled file size**: photos taken on recent mobile devices often exceed 5 to 10 MB each, which can block email delivery or exceed limits on online forms.
- **Risk of incompatibility**: some document management systems reject raw image formats (HEIC, large PNGs) but instantly accept standard PDFs.

Converting to PDF locks in the page order, guarantees a consistent layout across all screens, and simplifies archiving.

---

## Step 1: Prepare and Frame Your Image Captures

Before assembling your files, make sure each photo meets a few fundamental criteria:

1. **Even lighting**: prefer natural light while avoiding casting shadows over the document with your camera.
2. **Correct orientation**: rotate each image to the proper reading direction (portrait or landscape) prior to conversion.
3. **Clean cropping**: remove unnecessary margins around printed sheets or ID cards to maintain optimal legibility.

--- 

## Step 2: Assemble Your Images into a Single PDF Document

To convert your images directly from your computer or phone without installing paid software or risking file privacy, you can use the online tool [Images en PDF](https://jchub.dev/fr/outils/images-en-pdf).

This tool runs locally in your web browser, meaning your personal images and financial proofs are never transmitted to a third-party server.

### Step-by-Step Procedure:

- **File selection**: import all the images (JPG, JPEG, or PNG format) belonging to your file.
- **Reordering**: drag and drop the thumbnails to arrange each item in your desired chronological or logical order (e.g., ID card on page 1, proof of address on page 2).
- **Page format selection**: opt for a standard A4 format with automatic scaling to ensure visual consistency.
- **PDF generation**: download the assembled document in one click.

---

## Step 3: Check and Adjust File Size

Some corporate or institutional platforms limit attachment sizes to 2 MB or 5 MB. If your generated file turns out to be too large:

- Resize the original images to a moderate resolution (150 to 300 DPI is more than enough for text documents).
- Use grayscale captures for text-only documents (contracts, certificates).
- Pass the final document through a compression tool if necessary.

--- 

## Best Practices for a Reusable Professional File

- **Name your file clearly**: adopt an explicit naming convention such as `Nom_Prenom_Justificatifs_2026.pdf` rather than a generic name like `scan1.pdf`.
- **Keep an original copy**: maintain a structured folder containing your source images and the combined PDF on a secure storage drive.
- **Verify readability**: open the PDF document on a reference screen and zoom to 100% to ensure all text, signatures, and stamps are perfectly visible.

By applying these simple steps, you save valuable time during administrative procedures and ensure your submissions are processed quickly by your contacts.
